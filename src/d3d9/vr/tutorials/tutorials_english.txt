# HelpGeneralXBox
[message 0xBB215]
Left stick: Move, where you are looking
Right stick: Turn
Right stick pushed down and held: Your hotkeys, at your right hand

Right trigger: Attack. Swing a melee weapon with your arm; hold the trigger to charge a power attack
Left grip at the weapon: Hold it with both hands, which steadies your aim

A: Activate what your hand points at
Left trigger behind your back: Take ammunition for a reload, and push it into the weapon
X: With ammunition in your left hand, next ammunition type
B: Jump

Look at your left forearm: Pip-Boy 3000
Right grip at your left wrist, held: Pip-Boy light
Right grip at your right hip or behind your right shoulder: Holster or draw your weapon

Left stick (click): Sneak (Toggle)
Right grip: Grab with your free hand
Right stick (click): Vault-Tec Assisted Targeting System
Y: Wait; held, pause menu

# HelpAmmoXbox
[message 0x18D]
Ammunition is used by almost all Guns and Energy Weapons but is sometimes used by other weapon types. Basic ammo types have no special effects, but ammo subtypes may increase damage, reduce an enemy's Damage Threshold, or even decrease spread. Effects are listed on the ammo submenu when the ammo is highlighted. Most weapons can use any ammo subtype that starts with the same name as the base ammo. Common ammo subtypes include:

* Armor Piercing: Ignores a large portion of the enemy's Damage Threshold, does slightly more damage.

* Hollow Point: Does much more damage, but is easily blocked by armor.

* Over Charge: Used by Energy Weapons, increases damage but degrades the weapon more quickly.

* Slug: Used by shotguns, slugs replace buckshot with a single, large bullet that is more accurate.

To switch ammo subtypes, equip them on this menu. Outside of the Pip-Boy, take ammunition from behind your back with the left trigger and press X while your left hand holds it: pushing it into the weapon loads that type. With the manual reload off, the top cell of your hotkey wheel switches the type.

# HelpHotkeysXBox
[message 0x734EE]
You can hotkey inventory items and then use or equip them without accessing your Pip-Boy.

To hotkey an item in your Pip-Boy, highlight it, hold the right grip and push the left stick towards a hotkey slot.

Now when the Pip-Boy is closed, push the right stick down and hold it: the wheel of your hotkeys appears at your right hand. Move your hand towards a cell and let go of the stick to use or equip its item.
